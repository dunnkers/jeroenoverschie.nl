Onboard the LLM hype-train! 🚂 Are you curious about what LLMs (Large Language Models) can do for you? One of their use cases is to enrich datasets, by converting text into structured data. This can have huge benefits. Useful facts that were previously hidden inside a large piece of text can now be unveiled, allowing you to more accurately filter and query your data.
